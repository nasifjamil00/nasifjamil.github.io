ABOUT

Fieldwork first, interpretation second.

I'm a geologist and applied geophysicist based in Dhaka, currently serving as a Research Fellow in the Mining and Mineralogy Division at IMMM, BCSIR, where I work on mineral characterization and resource assessment. My path into the subsurface started with a BS and MS in Geology at the University of Dhaka, where my thesis combined seismic interpretation with geospatial techniques to track coastal change on Sonadia Island.

Since then my work has spread across three connected fronts: near-surface geophysical surveys (ERT, VES, MASW, seismic refraction), economic geology and mineral characterization using AAS, XRF, XRD and SEM-EDS, and geospatial analysis of land use, tectonics, and coastal dynamics using satellite imagery and GIS. I've contributed to five published papers and two more under review, spanning tectonics in the Rangpur Saddle to urban heat islands in Bogura.

Outside the lab and field, I led the University of Dhaka's Geophysical Society and Geo-Photography Club, and was selected for the Egypt SEG Marine Geophysics Field Camp — a reminder that good geoscience is still built on showing up in the field.
